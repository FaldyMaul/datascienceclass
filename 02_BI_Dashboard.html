Data dictionary — what do these words mean? tap to open
Word on this pagePlain meaning
ChurnThe customer left (stopped subscribing) last month. Churn rate = customers who left ÷ customers in the group.
ContractHow long the customer committed for. Month-to-month: can leave any time. One year / Two year: committed for that long.
Internet serviceThe technology. Fiber optic: glass cable, fast, newer. DSL: internet over the old copper telephone line, slower. No internet: phone line only.
TenureHow many months the person has been a customer.
PaymentHow the bill is paid. Electronic check: a manual online bank payment each month. Auto: charged automatically.
Tech supportA paid add-on: priority technical help.
Monthly chargeThe monthly bill, in US dollars (this sample is American).
